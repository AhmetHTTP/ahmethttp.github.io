Privacy policy

MotifHane was made by an individual developer for Van Olgunlaşma Enstitüsü, with the Institute's permission. This text explains what the game stores and where, and what information the ad and purchase components in the game send to Google.

There is no account. The game does not ask for your name, email or phone number. The game's own code does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, does not connect to the internet and does not send data anywhere. There are no servers and no analytics. You can play the game offline.

The game shows ads from AdMob, Google's ad service. The first 10 levels have no ads. After that, a full-screen ad may appear between levels, at most once every two levels. There are no ads while you play a level. When you finish a level for the first time, you can choose to watch a video to double that level's Akçe reward; the video never opens unless you ask for it. The No ads item removes the ads between levels; the optional video offer stays.

All ad requests are marked as directed to children. This means ads are not personalized, are not chosen by your interests and come only from ads suitable for a general audience. The game does not use or read the device's advertising ID.

To show ads, the AdMob component sends this information to Google: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's general location), interactions with the app and ads (app launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the component, and device identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses this information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The information is sent over an encrypted connection, may go to servers outside Türkiye and is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

The game has optional purchases. Purchases are made only through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles your payment details; they never reach the game. The game learns from the store that a purchase is complete and the purchase ID; it keeps them only on this device so that the same purchase is not given twice. Information the store collects is covered by the store's own privacy policy.

On Android, purchases go through Google's Google Play Billing Library. This library may send Google technical logs, such as whether purchase calls succeed and problems with the store connection. The app's internet permission is for this library, the ad component and parts of the game engine; the game's own code does not use it.

Your progress (finished levels, Akçe, lives, tools, Master Path, purchased items and settings) is stored on this device. The game never sends it anywhere and never shares it with anyone.

The game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. To pick a language, it only checks the device's language setting. The game itself does not send this setting off the device.

To delete your saved progress, uninstall the app. The game has no access to the information Google collects; for that information, the options in Google's privacy policy apply.

If this policy changes, the new text comes with an app update and on the same web page.
