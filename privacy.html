Privacy policy

MotifHane was made by an individual developer with the permission of Van Olgunlaşma Enstitüsü. This text explains what the game stores and where, and what information the ad, purchase and Google Play Games components in the game send to Google.

The game has no account of its own. The game does not ask for your name, email or phone number. On Android, the game asks for your year of birth when it first opens and does not save it; signing in to Google Play Games is optional (both below). The game's own code does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, does not connect to the internet and does not send data anywhere. There are no servers and no analytics. You can play the game offline.

The game shows ads from AdMob, Google's ad service. The first 10 levels have no ads. After that, a full-screen ad may appear between levels, at most once every two levels. There are no ads while you play a level. From level 11 on, when you finish a level for the first time, you can choose to watch a video to double that level's Akçe reward; the video never opens unless you ask for it. The No ads item removes the ads between levels; the optional video offer stays.

All ad requests are marked as directed to children. This means ads are not personalized, are not chosen by your interests and come only from ads suitable for a general audience. The game does not use or read the device's advertising ID.

To show ads, the AdMob component sends this information to Google: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's general location), interactions with the app and ads (app launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the component, and device identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses this information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The information is sent over an encrypted connection, may go to servers outside Türkiye and is covered by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

The game has optional purchases. Purchases are made only through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles your payment details; they never reach the game. The game learns from the store that a purchase is complete and the purchase ID; it keeps them only on this device so that the same purchase is not given twice. Information the store collects is covered by the store's own privacy policy.

On Android, purchases go through Google's Google Play Billing Library. This library may send Google technical logs, such as whether purchase calls succeed and problems with the store connection. The app's internet permission is for this library, the ad component, the Google Play Games component and parts of the game engine; the game's own code does not use it.

On Android, the game uses achievements from Google's Google Play Games service. This is optional and only available to players aged 13 or over. That is why the game asks for your year of birth when it first opens. Your year of birth is not saved; the device keeps only whether the answer means under 13 or 13 and over, and this is never sent anywhere. If the answer means under 13, the game never starts the Google Play Games component and the Achievements button does not appear. The answer does not change ads; ad requests are marked as directed to children for everyone. If you are 13 or over and already have a Play Games profile on the device, Play Games signs in automatically when the game opens. The game does not ask players without a profile to create one; the Achievements button in Settings opens Google's own screen only if you choose it. While you are signed in, the Google Play Games component sends the achievements you earn and progress counts (for example, the number of levels finished) to Google. Google links them to your Play Games profile and shows them in Google Play Games according to your profile's visibility settings. When you sign in, Google tells the Google component inside the game your Play Games ID; the game's own code does not read, store or share that ID, your gamer name or your profile picture. The component also sends analytics and diagnostics to Google to keep it stable and improve it. This information travels over an encrypted connection and is covered by Google's privacy policy; the game does not use it for advertising. For supervised Google Accounts, a Play Games profile needs a parent's approval. The game is fully playable without signing in and without internet.

Your progress (finished levels, Akçe, lives, tools, Master Path, purchased items, the result of the age question and settings) is stored on this device. The game never shares it with anyone; only when you are signed in to Google Play Games do achievements and progress counts go to Google as described above.

The game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. To pick a language, it only checks the device's language setting. The game itself does not send this setting off the device.

To delete your saved progress, uninstall the app. The game has no access to the information Google collects; for that information, the options in Google's privacy policy apply. You can delete achievements and your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings or your Google Account.

If this policy changes, the new text comes with an app update and on the same web page.
